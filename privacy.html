gmail-mcp — privacy policy

gmail-mcp is a personal tool used only by its developer, Eric Weber, to read and draft email in his own two Gmail accounts. It runs only on his own computer.

Gmail data is shown to him and the AI assistant he runs locally, and is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Sign-in tokens are stored in the Windows Credential Manager on that computer.

The app never sends email; drafts are reviewed and sent by hand.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
